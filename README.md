This will be a blog dedicated to research & labs that I do along the way. With this new approach I have been for barely 3 months. Although sometimes I can get to have ideas that seem complex connecting some dots with others, the challenge is that my current technical knowledge isn't always enough to actually build or execute them yet. But, as with my other repositories, I know that this can become a very good practice and help me (or force me) to write and learn more. They won't have the quality of elite blogs, but at the same time not writing them for that reason could be considered a mistake by many. So leaving aside shame or paralysis, I think it is fine to start with something that to many will seem simple, but which sometimes are details that we overlook.
